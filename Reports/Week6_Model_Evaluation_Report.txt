## Interpretation and limitations
- Baseline classification scores near chance indicate weak predictive performance.
- Negative regression R² values indicate worse performance than predicting the test-set mean.
- The claims model is only an Approved-versus-Pending alternative; the source has no Rejected labels.
- The high-risk target is a severity-derived proxy, not a clinically validated risk label.
- Satisfaction classes are derived from feedback ratings, not a separately supplied clinical outcome.
- Length-of-stay results use only appointment-linked admissions, a limited subset of the available admission records.
- Model metrics and feature-importance rankings do not establish causation or clinical usefulness.
- Do not use these baseline models to make real patient-care, insurance, or billing decisions.
- The length-of-stay model underperforms its baseline on the test set (MAE 2.4745 vs 2.3533; R2 -0.1554 vs -0.0098).

## Recorded target limitations
- Task: Hospital admission classification; Status: Not labelled in this script; Limitation: Admission sheet appointment IDs overlap only partially with Appointments; unmatched records cannot safely be labelled non-admissions.
- Task: Insurance claim outcome (3 classes); Status: Not trainable with current labels; Limitation: Observed claim statuses are Approved and Pending; no Rejected records were found.
- Task: High-risk classification; Status: Proxy target provided; Limitation: Severe diagnosis is used as a transparent proxy, not a clinically validated risk label.
- Task: Facility clustering; Status: Requires further validation; Limitation: Facility-to-department linkage rates in the audit were about 5%; avoid unsupported facility-level conclusions.
